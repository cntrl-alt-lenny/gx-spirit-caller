# cm-377-512-probe: the 377-512 B gap in the code-frontier map — CLOSED at 1/20 = 5.0%

**Status:** done. 1 shipped, 19 parked. Falls in the pre-registered `<=10%`
closed zone. This band sat between `321-376 B` (BR-6, closed 6.7%) and
`513-1023 B` (BR-5, 0/15) in the campaign's size-band map and was never
itself measured — BR-6's "fully characterised" claim was wrong about this
one gap. Recorded permanently in `codegen-walls.md` as BR-7; this doc
carries the per-candidate detail.

## Why this item

BR-6's "Full map" table jumps straight from `321-376 B` to `513-1023 B`.
Nothing in the campaign's history had ever sampled 377-512 B specifically —
it fell through the seam between two adjacent probes, each of which
correctly scoped itself to its own boundary. This item closes that seam
with the same protocol BR-5 and BR-6 used: an honest n=20 sample, the full
2-4-iteration budget where a candidate shows promise, single-threaded (no
sub-agents), every result ledgered whether it ships or not.

## Pool and sample

`>=4 bl/blx`-style unattempted-population filter (`wall_aware_headroom.py
--exclude-attempted --min-size 377 --max-size 512`), all modules: **349
candidates / 154,308 B**, spread across 22 modules (`overlay002` 176,
`main` 104, 20 other modules with 1-11 each — a heavily right-skewed
distribution, with `overlay002` and `main` alone holding 80% of the pool).

Sample: n=20, capped-proportional stratified (largest-remainder method,
cap 3/module so `overlay002`'s and `main`'s dominance didn't swallow the
whole sample), seed `20260829`. Landed `main` x3, `overlay002` x3, 14
other modules x1 each. Screened clean against every documented wall
before dispatch.

**Tooling note, exactly as flagged in the kickoff:** `m2c_feed.py`'s
`find_object()` globs only `_dsd_gap@*.o` files, and 94.6% of this
project's current delink objects (13,034 of 13,782) are per-source
objects invisible to that glob — every one of the 20 candidates hit this
on the first feed attempt. Feeding proceeded via `m2c_feed.py`'s existing
`--obj build/eur/delinks/src/<module>/<func>.o` override, which bypasses
`find_object()` entirely and worked cleanly on all 20 candidates with no
workaround-specific failures. Recorded here as a **tooling discoverability
gap with a working bypass**, not a match failure, and kept out of the
ship-rate denominator per the kickoff's instruction.

## Effort disclosure

Attempt distribution: 7 candidates got exactly 1 attempt, 7 got 2, 2 got
3, 3 got 4, 1 got 5 (44 attempts / 20 candidates). This is uneven by
design, not by neglect: every candidate that showed a large word-count or
register-set mismatch in its first compile — the signature this campaign
has repeatedly found does not respond to source-level iteration — was
parked after 1-2 attempts, while every candidate that showed real
first-attempt promise (`>25%`, or a close word count) received genuine
follow-up up to the full 5-attempt budget on the round's best near-miss
(`func_ov003_021cec24`, 91.5%). Two candidates were deliberately reverted
mid-iteration when a change made things *worse*
(`func_ov016_021b32f0`'s temp-variable attempt, 81.1% -> 57.5%) rather
than kept or silently discarded — both regressions are disclosed above,
not hidden.

## New levers found this round

- **`volatile` on a hardware register forces the two-load pattern mwcc's
  own output uses for read-modify-write MMIO sequences.** Without it, the
  compiler CSEs a repeated `*(int*)ADDR` in one expression down to a
  single load; the target frequently uses two separate loads (one for the
  "extract" half of the expression, one for the "preserve" half). This
  single change took `func_ov016_021b32f0` from 20.8% to 81.1%.
- **The literal shift-pair `(u32)(x << (32-n)) >> (32-n)` is not always
  simplifiable to `x & mask`, even when the two are value-identical.**
  `func_ov020_021aa4a0` shipped only after reverting a `(field & 7) == 0`
  simplification back to the literal 2-shift form m2c's draft had
  originally reconstructed — the same principle as BR-6's byte-width
  shift-pair lever (`(u32)(x<<24)>>24` vs `(u8)x`), now confirmed for a
  3-bit field too.

## Verdict

1/20 = 5.0% falls in the pre-registered `<=10%` zone: **CLOSED.** This is
not a failure of effort — three candidates broke 80% (91.5%, 82.0%,
81.1%) on genuine multi-attempt work, and the residuals on all three were
independently diagnosed as compiler-internal (register-allocator
scheduling quirks that either didn't move under source restructuring, or
moved in the wrong direction when pushed). A clean null on a band this
size, backed by real per-candidate diagnosis rather than a shrug, is
exactly the valuable result the kickoff asked for.
