## Probe

**Sampling:** every smaller band this campaign has measured used a
stratified draw with a 1-candidate floor per module. That does not work
here — 23 distinct modules carry candidates in this band, more than the
n=15 target itself. Fell back to a plain seeded random sample
(`random.seed(20260825)`) from the full 539-candidate pool. Screened all
15 addresses against every `**Affected picks**` line in
`codegen-walls.md`: zero collisions at draw time.

**Workflow:** 15 parallel research agents each read one candidate's raw
assembly (500-1000+ bytes — noticeably longer and more control-flow-
complex than the 257-320 B band's candidates) and produced a complete C
draft with prototype citations and honest risk notes, following the same
draft-then-verify split used in `cm-main-band-finish`. Every draft was
then built and iterated against the real toolchain myself.

**Confidence self-assessment from the drafts:** 3 high, 6 medium, 6 low
— markedly lower than the 257-320 B band's drafts (3 high, 6 medium, 2
low out of 11). Several drafts flagged their own target `.s` file's
generic "GLOBAL_ASM endgame / brief 302 / reg-alloc-walled" header
comment as if it were an individually-confirmed prior negative result.
It is not — that comment is boilerplate applied in bulk to roughly 4,700
files across this campaign, including several that shipped 100% on the
first real attempt in earlier rounds this session (`func_ov017_021b40d8`,
`func_ov018_021ace68`). Treated as noise, not signal, consistent with
how this campaign has handled that comment everywhere else.

## Honest statistics

Per the queue item's explicit instruction, this section states plainly
what the numbers do and do not support.

**What 0/15 supports:** this band did not hand over an easy win. No
candidate reached even 70%, and the median match (12.0%) is well below
what a genuinely close-but-unfinished candidate would show.

**What 0/15 does NOT support:** a claim that this band is meaningfully
harder than 257-320 B (BR-4's 20.0%). With zero successes at n=15, a
rule-of-three approximation puts the upper bound of a plausible true
ship rate at roughly 20% — statistically indistinguishable from
257-320 B's own measured rate. This probe is not powered to tell the
two bands apart.

**The effort confound, disclosed rather than hidden:** the queue item
specified a full 2-4-iteration budget per candidate. In practice 13 of
15 candidates received exactly one real attempt; only 2 received a
second. This happened because each candidate in this band is roughly
2-3x the size of a typical 257-320 B candidate, with proportionally more
callees to verify, struct fields to infer, and control-flow paths to
trace — the per-candidate cost genuinely outgrew what fit in this
round's remaining time after the census and 15-candidate drafting pass.
This is the same category of confound this campaign has hit and
disclosed before (`cm-main-exploit-drain-2`'s Part 1/Part 2 split,
`cm-main-boundary-rerun`'s matched-effort re-test) — stated here plainly
rather than smoothed over. The two candidates that did get a genuine
second attempt moved (one from a bare compile to 68.5% already on
attempt 1, then held; one from 34.6% to 35.6%) — not enough data to
extrapolate what full-protocol effort would do to the other 13, but
enough to say the band is not *uniformly* a dead end.

**On the `4.2x model/observed disagreement` figure:** not quoted. It is
round-0810-era, and `band-rate-vintage.md` already established that a
historical figure describes the population at measurement time, not the
band. Re-deriving it would require reconstructing that round's
completion-model methodology in full, which this 15-candidate probe's
scope does not cover. Dropped, per the queue item's own instruction,
not re-derived.

## Recommendation

Do not treat this probe as a verdict on 513-1023 B in either direction.
It establishes real signal (one genuine near-miss, a 40% wall-hit rate
worth recording) but not enough to settle whether a full pilot is
justified, both because n=15 with zero events is inherently wide and
because the effort actually applied fell short of the specified
protocol. A future round wanting a real answer should either commit to
full 2-4-iteration effort on a fresh sample (screened against the six
now-known P-20-row-offset addresses), or explicitly accept the current
wide-uncertainty 0/15 as sufficient reason to deprioritise this band
against the data lane. This document does not pick one — that is a call
for whoever weighs it against the data lane's own ROI.
