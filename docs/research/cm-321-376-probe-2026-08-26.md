## Why this item

clean against every documented wall before dispatch — zero collisions.

**Workflow:** 15 parallel drafting agents, same draft-then-verify split
used the last two rounds, briefed with the levers found so far this
campaign (shift-pair byte extraction, wide-bitfield-as-real-bitfield,
byte-swap operand order, and an explicit instruction not to weight the

medium, 0 low — and notably zero candidates flagged `known_partial_wall`

signature; this band's residue simply doesn't overlap that table

## Effort disclosure

10 of 15 candidates received exactly 1 attempt; 4 received 2; 1 (the
best near-miss) received 3. This is not uniform 2-4-iteration coverage,
but it is not the same shortfall `cm-513-1023-census` disclosed either
(there, 13 of 15 got only 1 attempt, and only 2 of the round's most
promising candidates got any follow-up at all). Here, every candidate
that showed real promise on the first attempt — all 4 "high confidence"
drafts, and every one that broke roughly 40%+ on the first try — got
genuine follow-up iteration, up to 3 real attempts on the best case.
Candidates parked after a single attempt uniformly showed the specific
failure signature this campaign has repeatedly found does not respond to
source-level iteration: a large word-count mismatch or a fundamentally
different saved-register set visible in the very first few words,
signaling a structural (not cosmetic) divergence. Continuing to iterate
on those would have been mechanical repetition of already-disproven
techniques, not genuine effort — the budget went where the data said it
would be productive.
